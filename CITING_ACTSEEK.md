Currently available algorithms enable mining of proteins based on their sequence or structure. However, activities of many proteins, such as enzymes and drug targets, are dictated by active site residues and their surroundings rather than the overall structure or sequence of a protein.We introduce ActSeek—a computer vision-inspired fast program—that searches structural databases for proteins with active sites similar to the seed protein. ActSeek is implemented to mine proteins with desired active site environments from the Alphafold database.
